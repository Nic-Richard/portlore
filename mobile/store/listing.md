## Full description

Explore your next port with Portlore.

Find cruise ports, choose your terminal and plan your day ashore. Browse guides to
sights, food, shops and outdoor stops, view places on the map and build an itinerary.

- Search ports or use your location to find one nearby.
- Read port guides and choose stops for your day.
- Keep recently opened guides available offline.
- Open directions in Google Maps.
- Set a sail-away reminder on your phone.

Guides can be read offline after opening them. Maps, photos, weather and online
searches need a connection. Location and notification permissions are optional.
If location is declined, distances use the selected terminal instead.

Portlore is free to use, with no in-app purchases or account requirement. Check
opening times, travel conditions and your ship's departure time before setting out;
generated guide information can be incomplete or out of date.
